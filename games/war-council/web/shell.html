War Council

Command three generals in a real-time battle. Each order you send is read by Claude, which decides how every general responds and how the Red army answers.

  • Take the Red banner at (21,8) and hold it for 3 turns, or rout every Red unit.
  • Give orders in plain English, such as Old Qin, hold the middle bridge (12,8) or Huo Lie, hit E2 from the north bridge!
  • Your tone counts. Praise raises a general's morale and bond; insults can make them refuse you.
  • Click the map to insert a coordinate, click an enemy to insert its ID, click a general to address them alone.

Orders use your own Claude usage, and the first order asks your permission. If Claude is unavailable, a simple rule engine and a scripted enemy play instead.
